Demonstrable Core Business Workflows

Interactive end-to-end flows ready for mentors, teammates, and clients

Workflow A

Walk-in Cashier Purchase & Thermal Slip

Cashier Sopheak Lim opens POS at Phnom Penh Central (B001), scans Anker 20W charger, tenders cash, receipt RCP-2026-xxx prints, physical stock drops from 18 to 17, and dashboard KPIs reflect the $18 revenue.

Workflow B

Online Delivery Order & Reservation

Customer places delivery order online. Branch B001 available stock drops immediately via reservation. Fulfillment staff marks "Picking" → "Dispatched", which fulfills the reservation and deducts physical on-hand stock.

Workflow C

Branch Click & Collect Store Pickup

Customer selects "Store Pickup" at Sen Sok (B003). System validates local stock. Branch staff picks the item and marks it "Ready for Pickup" for customer handover.

Workflow D

Procurement & Goods Receiving

Procurement creates PO-2026-014. PO creation does NOT increase inventory. Head Office approves. Inventory staff receives 40 chargers into Central Warehouse (WH001), increasing active on-hand stock.

Workflow E

Inter-Branch Stock Transfer

Sen Sok (B003) is low on Logitech mice. Manager submits transfer request from Toul Kork (B002). Upon dispatch, source stock decreases. Destination confirms receiving, and Sen Sok stock increases.

Workflow H

Bakong KHQR Payment & Verification

Simulate authentic National Bank of Cambodia KHQR payments on POS and online store with live 180s countdown and dual-currency (USD/KHR). If payment expires or fails, reserved stock is automatically rolled back!
